\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\begin{otherlanguage}{english}
In early 2024, a research line was established within the Atmospheric Physics Group to investigate the possible existence of microbial life in liquid veins within ice. Its initial stages examined the motion of microorganisms from stagnant water samples collected in Córdoba between 0 °C and 15 °C, with an interest in their activity near the melting point of ice. These movements were recorded using a camera coupled to an optical microscope. Four types of aquatic microorganisms were distinguished in the initial stagnant water samples. Studying their trajectories, mean speeds, and possible relationship with temperature motivated the need for individual measurements.

Manual trajectory reconstruction of these microorganisms using \textit{Tracker} requires recording positions across numerous frames. To support this analysis, this work developed a computer vision system integrating a YOLO11 detector adapted to the available material, BoT-SORT tracking, and position recording by identifier for spherical microorganisms. From these positions, the system computes distances, speed, changes in speed, and temporal indicators, applies a rule-based classification of motion patterns, and generates outputs for inspection.

The evaluation includes records from two training runs, controlled tests, and a comparison between one automated trajectory and a partial manual reference.

The computational contribution lies in integrating and adapting the processing workflow to the material of this experimental research line. Tracking evidence is limited to the studied case, corresponding to one spherical microorganism; broader evaluation, validation for the other microorganisms present in the water samples, and measurement of time savings remain pending. The thesis does not evaluate temperature effects on motion. Measurements remain in px, px/s, and px/s$^2$; conversion to physical units belongs to a stage outside the scope of this work.
\end{otherlanguage}
